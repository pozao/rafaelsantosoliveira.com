What I Review

Five areas, one clear picture

Master Data

The quality and consistency of your customers, suppliers, items, and BOMs, and whether you have rules in place to keep them clean.

Business Processes

How your core workflows actually run in B1, and where workarounds have crept in.

Users & Training

How confidently your team uses the system, where adoption is weak, and whether you have super users who can support everyone else.

Compliance & Audit Readiness

For FDA-regulated companies, a gap review of your validation documentation and electronic records controls against 21 CFR Part 11.

Support & Roadmap

Your partner and support setup, your add-ons, and how ready you are for upgrades and growth.
